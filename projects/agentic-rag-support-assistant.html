Overview

An agentic RAG-powered support assistant that autonomously resolves technical product queries using LLM-driven reasoning, replacing rigid rule-based responses with retrieval-augmented, context-aware answers generated in real time from relevant product knowledge.

Purpose — Why This Project Exists

Rule-based support bots break the moment a question doesn't match a pre-written pattern, forcing users into frustrating dead ends or a support queue. This assistant exists to replace that brittle logic with an agent that actually retrieves the relevant product knowledge for a query and reasons over it — giving real-time, knowledge-grounded answers instead of a canned response.
