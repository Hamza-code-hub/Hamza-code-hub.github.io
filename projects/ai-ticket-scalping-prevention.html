Overview

An AI-based fraud detection and dynamic pricing system for ticketing platforms. Anomaly detection models identify bot activity and suspicious transactions, while pricing and fraud-prevention mechanisms work together for fairer ticket distribution — with CI/CD workflows automating model retraining, testing and deployment to production.

Purpose — Why This Project Exists

Bots can buy up high-demand event tickets within seconds of release, leaving genuine fans to pay inflated resale prices. This system exists to spot that bot-like purchasing behavior in real time — abnormal transaction velocity, patterns and account signals — and respond with fraud blocking and dynamic pricing defenses instead of letting scalpers walk away with the entire inventory.
